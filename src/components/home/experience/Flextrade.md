# FlexTrade

## Software Developer

At FlexTrade, I was on the UI team working on the [FlexOne OMS system](https://flextrade.com/products/flexone-order-execution-management-system/). OMS stands for Order Management System, and the product enabled finacial customers (primarily hudge funds and pension providers) with the ability to manage orders of equities, futures, bonds and much more through customised portfolios.

The OMS is highly integrated with [FlexTRADER EMS](https://flextrade.com/products/flextrader-execution-management-system/) (hence the OEMS), so while my work didn't deal with the extreme high precision required by the EMS, I did have to make sure that my work integrated well with the EMS and remained responsive and effective enough for customers who were used to high performance.

The primary OMS system is a desktop application - built using Scala and fronted with Java Swing. To aid UI development, we also used multiple swing-based libraries, most notably JGoodies and Jide. FlexTrade was also developing a mobile and a web-based OMS application (built with React and TypeScript). While I didn't get as many opportunities to work on this as I would've liked - since it had a much lower priority than the web application - I was still able to have an impact on the future on the application. I did this by suggesting and implementing react-based best practices as well leading a TypeScript study group, helping upskill my colleagues in web development.

When I was there, FlexTrade was a mid-size company, of 500-1000 employees across offices in US, UK, India and Singapore. While the UI team I worked with was based in London, we also worked closely with the backend team that was based in Milwaukee, US. I joined FlexTrade in September 2025, where I worked hybridly - 3 days a week in their London office and 2 days a week from home.
